Fast judgment requirements for analyze_only:

- Scores are 1-100 integers. convictionScore is the final agent judgment, not just the mechanical radar score.
- Keep verdict, whyItWorks, whyItMightFail, and bestAngle to one short sentence each.
- Return only the analyze_only JSON shape when intent is analyze_only.

Do not mention IP, trademarks, legal exposure, implied endorsement, public-figure consent, brand association, or internal capture/technical limitations.

- If launchFitScore is 70 or higher, still reject if the joke is not repeatable or the social energy is shallow.
